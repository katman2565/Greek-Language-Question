# Comparing the Histories of the Greek Language Question: Towards a Computer-Assisted Analysis
A computational methodology to analyze three key histories of the Greek Language Question. We examine origins and periodizations, the separation of historical narration from quotation, the evaluative framing of mentioned scholars, recurrent themes and emotions, semantic search and corpus-specific word embeddings. The analysis traces the historians’ citation practices, showing how earlier ideas are enregistered in their narratives, creating an ideological space for what came to be known as The Greek Language Question.  
This repository contains Jupyter notebooks developed for a computational study of historical writings related to the Greek Language Question.

The notebooks explore different aspects of the texts, including preprocessing, semantic similarity, word embeddings, emotion analysis, and the identification and evaluative framing of historical figures.

### Notebooks

Preprocess_Kordatos: Text preprocessing for Kordatos.

Preprocess_Megas: Text preprocessing for Megas.

Preprocess_Sathas: Text preprocessing for Sathas.

Scholars_detection_&_quotes_extraction: Identification of named scholars and extraction of quotations.

Scholar_evaluative_framing: LLM-assisted analysis of how scholars are evaluated and represented in the texts.

Emotional_analysis: Analysis of emotional characteristics in the texts.

Encoding_&_Clustering: Text encoding and clustering.

Similarity_search_&_Idea_Extraction: Semantic similarity search and extraction of relevant ideas.

Word_embeddings: Exploration of semantic relationships through word embeddings.

#### Notes

The notebooks document different stages of an exploratory research workflow. Some require external datasets, pretrained models or API access and may need adjustments to run in a different environment.
The repository is intended to provide an overview of the computational methods used in the research.
